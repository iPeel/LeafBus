How to read it

linker the bus runs through (the minimum set) optional linker: panels touch here; more power paths and a stiffer assembly, the panels ignore the extra data loop controller (LeafBus ESP32 or the Nanoleaf controller) power supply
hops from the controller: few → many

Data and power both travel panel to panel through linkers. Every hop is a relay: fewer hops from the controller to the farthest panel make the bus quicker to enumerate and more robust; fewer hops from a supply mean less voltage drop. Controller and supplies plug into free connectors on the outside edge.
